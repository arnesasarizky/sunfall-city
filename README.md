Sunfall City is an original top-down 2D open-world crime game that runs in the browser. Juno Marchetti runs jobs for fixer Odette "Odie" Crane against the Glass Eels in a golden-hour coastal city: explore on foot or steal any parked car, outrun a five-star wanted system with pursuing cruisers, and work through a five-mission story arc — deliveries, a pier pickup, a police escape, a dockside shootout and a timed finale. Seeded procedural city with traffic, pedestrians, a working minimap, synthesized WebAudio sound, and a localStorage career save. No build step and no assets: everything is drawn and synthesized in code.

## How to play

You are Juno, a driver-for-hire in Sunfall City. Take jobs from Odie, earn cash, and stay ahead of the law.

- **Objective:** finish all five story missions — a delivery run, a pier pickup, a police escape, a dockside shootout, and a timed finale — then keep the city as your playground.
- **On foot:** walk the streets, talk to contacts, and hop into any parked car.
- **In a car:** drive fast, but the law notices. Your wanted level runs from one to five stars; at higher stars, cruisers chase and roadblock you.
- **Fighting:** shoot or fight your way out when a job turns sour, then lose the heat before the next pickup.
- **Progress:** your career, cash and completed missions save in the browser automatically.

### Controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| W / A / S / D or arrows | Move on foot / drive |
| E | Interact — talk, enter or exit a car, advance dialogue |
| Mouse or J | Fire weapon |
| K | Melee attack |
| P | Pause |
| M | Mute sound |

## Features

- Open-world coastal city, procedurally generated from a seed — same streets every run, new discoveries each time.
- Five-mission story arc with dialogue, plus free-roam driving and on-foot exploration.
- Five-star wanted system with pursuing police cruisers and roadblocks.
- Live traffic, pedestrians, and a working minimap.
- Synthesized WebAudio sound effects and music — no audio files.
- Career save in localStorage: cash, missions and progress persist between sessions.
- Zero build step, zero downloaded assets: open the page and play.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open the printed local address in a browser. No install step: the game is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript served as static files.
